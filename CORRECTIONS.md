# Verified corrections (fact-check 2026-10-28) — apply to data.js

1. Exam: NO official time limit (≈45 min is only typical). Never claim a hard time limit.
2. Exam: since 1.4.2025 there is also a question type where you choose the correct PICTURE (Abbildungsauswahlaufgabe). Mention it in exam mechanics.
3. Exam tip: 5-point questions are very often Vorfahrt questions -> Vorfahrt deserves the most study time (two wrong 5-pointers = fail regardless of total).
4. Exam tip: do NOT tick all options blindly in "Womit müssen Sie rechnen?" — a single wrong tick makes the whole question wrong. Tick only realistic dangers.
5. Parking: no parking 5 m before/after Kreuzungen/Einmündungen; if a baulich angelegter Radweg runs on the right: 8 m BEFORE the Kreuzung/Einmündung.
6. Haltestelle (Z. 224): 15 m before/after = PARKING forbidden; short halting is allowed if nobody is obstructed.
7. Fußgängerüberweg: 5 m before it halting AND parking forbidden.
8. Warndreieck: innerorts ≈ 50 m, Landstraße ≈ 100 m, Autobahn mindestens 150 m (150–400 m). Replace any "200 m".
9. Bus/Schulbus standing at Haltestelle with Warnblinklicht: pass only with Schrittgeschwindigkeit and at a distance that endangers no passenger; this also applies to Gegenverkehr ON THE SAME FAHRBAHN. Bus approaching a Haltestelle with Warnblinklicht: overtaking forbidden.
10. Lichthupe/Hupe to announce overtaking: ONLY außerorts. Innerorts only to warn of danger.
11. Alcohol breakdown: about 0.1–0.2 ‰ per hour (count with 0.1 to be safe); coffee, fresh air, cold shower do not help.
12. Grünpfeil: Z. 720 (Blechschild for all) = first stop, then turn right carefully, let pedestrians/cyclists pass; Z. 721 = Grünpfeil only for cyclists.
13. Rettungsgasse: Autobahn and außerorts roads with ≥ 2 lanes per direction, as soon as traffic moves at Schrittgeschwindigkeit or stands — not only when Blaulicht comes.
14. Schrittgeschwindigkeit: no legal number; courts ≈ up to 7 km/h -> teach "4–7 km/h, wie ein Fußgänger".
15. Richtgeschwindigkeit 130 = recommendation; faster is not an offence but may cause Mitschuld in an accident.
16. Pattern honesty: the wording patterns are trainer experience, not law. Label them "خبرة مجرَّبة" and keep the exceptions visible. The law-backed "nur" examples are: Schall-/Leuchtzeichen nur außerorts beim Überholen oder bei Gefahr; Nebelschlussleuchte nur bei Sichtweite unter 50 m; Handy nur bei vollständig ausgeschaltetem Motor; Autobahn nur für Fahrzeuge > 60 km/h.
